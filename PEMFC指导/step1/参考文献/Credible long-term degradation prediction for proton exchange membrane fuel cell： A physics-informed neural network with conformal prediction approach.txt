The insufficient durability of Proton Exchange Membrane Fuel Cells (PEMFCs) hinders their commercialization, making accurate long-term prognosis essential. However, existing methods struggle to balance physical interpretability, accuracy, and uncertainty quantification. This paper proposes a novel framework, Physics-Informed Neural Network with Conformal Prediction (PINNCP). First, to address black-box limitations, we develop a gray-box modeling paradigm that integrates physical mechanisms with data. Instead of directly fitting voltage curves, a neural network adaptively learns the meta-parameters of a generalized power-law model. By embedding electrochemical equations into the loss function, the method accurately inverts and decouples key internal physical states without parameter labels. Second, to quantify risks, a physics-informed calibrated conformal prediction strategy is introduced. By calibrating nonconformity scores with physical constraints, it generates prediction intervals with strict statistical guarantees and physical consistency. Extensive validation under static, quasi-dynamic, and dynamic conditions demonstrates that PINNCP accurately predicts long-term voltage degradation trajectories and provides high-quality confidence intervals. Furthermore, by leveraging extracted physical parameters, the method enables degradation prediction transfer across same-type PEMFCs, significantly enhancing practical applicability.
