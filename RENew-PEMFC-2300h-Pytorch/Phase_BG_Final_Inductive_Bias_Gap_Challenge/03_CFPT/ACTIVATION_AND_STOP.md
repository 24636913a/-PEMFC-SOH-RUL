# CFPT — activated for source and protocol gate only

CFPT was activated after PDFNet exited at its source gate and xLSTMTime exited
at its synthetic resource gate. Its official source and protocol gate were then
audited before any Development performance was produced.

The gate found `CFPT_PROTOCOL_INCOMPATIBLE`: official CFPT requires and uses
decoder future timestamps, whereas the frozen official TimesNet forecasting
body does not functionally consume those future marks. No compliant Route A or
Route B comparison can therefore be constructed without a prohibited source or
Raw-F2 change. No CFPT model was constructed, preflighted, trained, scored, or
evaluated on Test.
