# Model novelty and decomposition boundary audit

This phase is a preregistered backbone comparison, not a decomposition experiment. TimesNet retains its internal FFT period discovery; SimpleTM retains its official internal SWT/reconstruction and GeomAttention; DUET retains temporal routing and channel soft clustering; ModernTCN retains official convolutional hierarchy. These internal computations are architecture operations.

Network-external VMD, EMD, EEMD, CEEMDAN, wavelet features, modal features, SG changes, and feature reselection are prohibited in this phase. A later, separately preregistered input-representation study may compare Raw F2 with causally fit EMD/VMD-derived representations after the winner is fixed. It must never decompose the full 2300 h record before splitting.

SimpleTM is therefore never relabelled as a no-wavelet model, and its internal SWT is not evidence that external modal feature engineering occurred.
