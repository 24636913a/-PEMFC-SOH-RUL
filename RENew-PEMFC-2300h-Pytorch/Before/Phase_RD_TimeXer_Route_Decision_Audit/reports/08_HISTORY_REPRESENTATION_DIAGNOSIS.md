# History representation diagnosis

B varies across histories and horizons, and P1/P2 have identical B under identical histories. B-only RMSE is 192.383 mV, but this does not measure an independently trained history-only model: B was optimized jointly with C and can be numerically compensated by C. The present evidence therefore neither proves history encoding ineffective nor identifies a specific history-backbone defect. Testing an independent history model would require a separate authorized training protocol.
