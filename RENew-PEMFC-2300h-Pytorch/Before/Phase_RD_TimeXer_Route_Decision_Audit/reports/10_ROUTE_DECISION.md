# Route decision

## Verified facts

- Frozen OPR B+C and P1/P2 squared-error identities reproduce from Development caches.
- P2 improves many ordinary/state-correct points, while rare predicted-mixed / true-nonmixed events can create extreme harmful corrections.
- Fold 1 / 12 h is dominated by a few targets; Fold 3 harm spans four segments and is therefore not the same concentration pattern.
- Alignment/index error is not supported by TTD; MR did not surpass P2 at its preregistered condition gate.

## Unresolved competition

At rare state errors, the saved cache cannot separate misclassified state probability, correlated current-magnitude forecast error, and the frozen FiLM correction response. B-only error also cannot identify independent history-only ability.

## Decision

**INCONCLUSIVE.** There is no single separable code-level mechanism that distinguishes all alternatives and no authorization for a new model experiment. A future protocol may test a falsifiable task-information hypothesis only after separately establishing forecastability and a causal target definition; it must not be presented as a proven TimeXer architecture remedy.
