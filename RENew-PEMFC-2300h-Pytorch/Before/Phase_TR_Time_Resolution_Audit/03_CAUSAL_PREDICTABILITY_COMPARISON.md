# Causal future-state predictability comparison

Fixed before validation: history is 24 h, horizon is 12 h; 30-minute = 48 to 24 steps and 10-minute = 144 to 72 steps. Inputs are only historical q, current mean and current standard deviation. Fold-local StandardScaler, Ridge(alpha=1), and class-balanced LogisticRegression(C=1) are fit on Fold train windows only. No future true current, voltage, or q is provided, and no calibration search occurs.
