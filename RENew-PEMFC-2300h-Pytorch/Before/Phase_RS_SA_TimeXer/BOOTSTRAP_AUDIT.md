# Paired time-block bootstrap

The statistic is the unweighted mean of three fold RMSEs. Pairs were resampled by common contiguous origin blocks, 2,000 repetitions, seed 42. A CI that includes zero is labeled `IMPROVEMENT_NOT_STATISTICALLY_RESOLVED`. Complete results are in `outputs/bootstrap/bootstrap_main_metric.csv` and `outputs/bootstrap/bootstrap_block_sensitivity.csv`.
