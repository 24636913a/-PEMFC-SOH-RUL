# Research question and claim boundary

RQ1 asks whether the frozen P1S affine predictor outperforms the frozen `NOMINAL_AGE_AFFINE_BASELINE` at nominal 2000 h and 2300 h. Nominal ageing labels are not exact polarization operating-age timestamps. This is late-life temporal generalization and incremental information beyond nominal age, not RUL. Even a supported result may only be called late-life temporal Test support for a calibrated degradation-related health proxy; it is not validated SOH.
