# Claim Boundary and Research Contribution

Candidate V1 is technically resolved but remains unexecuted and is not an
authorization to train. Its HOU median-of-three anchor functional and
uncertainty-aware sparse supervision do not alter the PAA/PAV claim boundary.

## What this protocol may investigate

The planned experiment asks whether a fixed-state operating-voltage observation
from raw duty-cycle reconstruction can support a smooth, directionally
consistent, uncertainty-quantified **Cycle-Aligned Latent Health State** when
it is sparsely aligned to Development polarization measurements.

The proposed contribution is the supervision structure and data-scale design:

1. raw duty-cycle reconstruction;
2. a fixed-state operating-voltage health observation;
3. sparse polarization anchoring;
4. monotone cycle latent dynamics; and
5. uncertainty-aware sparse supervision.

It does not claim a new backbone network.

## What may not be claimed

CHM must not call `H_c` a validated SOH, continuous health ground truth, a
causal degradation indicator, EOL, or RUL. Neither PAA nor PAV eliminated all
condition confounding: PAA matching supports a directional robustness statement
but had no preregistered distance cutoff or proof of full multivariable balance.

PAV temporally verified 6/6 Development alignments. It also qualified the
anchor estimand: the frozen PAA median source is directly within complete-cycle
joint common support for 3/6 anchors only. The primary model therefore uses all
six anchors with uncertainty-aware sparse supervision and separately reports
S1; it may not silently drop or re-median the other three.

A Development qualification would support only a Development latent-state
estimation result. Test validation is required before discussing an estimated
SOH name, and a separate later decision is required before any RUL research.
